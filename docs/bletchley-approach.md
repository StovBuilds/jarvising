**Subject:** An interactive Enigma teardown, built for the hut — free, sourced, and yours if useful

Dear Bletchley Park Learning team,

I'm Jack Stovell, founder of Adapt Progress Evolve, a small UK studio. Over the
past month we've built an interactive Enigma piece that I think belongs at
Bletchley more than it belongs on our website, so I'm writing to offer it.

It's a browser page, no install. You scroll and an Enigma I comes apart on a
desk in Hut 6 on the night watch: the oak case lifts into six labelled layers,
a rotor explodes into its own stack, one keypress is traced as current through
the plugboard, rotors and reflector, and the rotors step like an odometer.
Two chapters then tell the story of the people who broke it, from Rejewski and
the Pyry meeting to the bombes and the Wrens who ran them, with a bombe running
against the wall. The last beat is a faithful, typeable Enigma I: rotors
I·II·III, reflector B, ten plug pairs, double-step included. It never gives you
your own letter back.

- The piece: https://jarvising.com/projects/enigma/live/
- What it does and how it was made: https://jarvising.com/projects/enigma/
- A kiosk build for a gallery screen (attract loop, touch-first, works offline):
  https://jarvising.com/projects/enigma/live/?kiosk=1
- Every historical claim with its source and what we changed after checking:
  https://github.com/StovBuilds/jarvising/blob/main/docs/enigma-sources.md
- The source, MIT licensed: https://github.com/StovBuilds/jarvising

Three things I'd value from you, in whatever order suits:

1. **Corrections.** We checked the history against your own site, GCHQ, TNMOC
   and Crypto Museum, but your curators will see what we can't. Anything wrong
   gets fixed.
2. **A trial.** If a touch screen in the Learning space, or a link from your
   online resources, would be useful, we'll host it, adapt it (a Bletchley
   version with your framing, your branding, no ours) and support it, at no
   cost.
3. **A conversation.** Twenty minutes with whoever owns digital interpretation
   would tell us whether this is useful to you and what would make it more so.

There's no catch. Adapt Progress Evolve builds things like this to show what
small teams can do now; the point of this one was always the machine and the
people in the huts, and it's finished enough to be worth offering.

With thanks for everything the Trust does,

Jack Stovell
Founder, Adapt Progress Evolve
https://adaptprogressevolve.com · https://jstov.uk

## If they reply

- **Yes to a look / call:** offer three slots; prepare a five-minute walkthrough
  (title → lid card → assembly → rotor → path → hut → bombe → typing), and the
  kiosk build on a laptop in Chrome `--kiosk`.
- **Corrections:** each goes into `docs/enigma-sources.md` with the correction
  and who supplied it; ship within a day; tell them it's live.
- **Trial:** the Bletchley version is a config, not a fork: framing copy,
  credit line, hidden links, their logo in the chrome. Agree the idle timings
  and sound policy for the space. Hardware soak test before install.
- **No / silence after four weeks:** one polite follow-up with anything new
  (e.g. a Bletchley-version preview), then leave it. The piece stays public.
