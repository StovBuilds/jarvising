Adapt Progress Evolve

Adapt Progress Evolve is a software studio in London. We design, build and run our own portfolio of digital products, and take on selective client work: AI agents, Claude and LLM integration, and MVP builds.

Day to day, a fleet of specialised Claude AI agents does the work with human direction, and a person approves anything customer-facing, destructive or costly. These are the projects we run.

The studio's own site is adaptprogressevolve.com.

Writing and voice

  • ScriptGrain: Reads writing you've already published into a voice profile of 45 measured attributes, then drafts new content and scores each draft against it.
  • Dead Writing Theory: Tests with data, one case at a time, the claim that the web's writing is collapsing into one machine-made voice.
  • Brands over time: How 209 UK brands write, measured across 27,062 of their own pages from 2015 to 2026, before and after ChatGPT.

Being found by AI

  • Visibility to AI: Measures whether ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity name and cite a company when its buyers ask them questions.
  • What is GEO: A practical, source-linked beginner's guide to Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO), for business owners and marketers.
  • Hilyt: Open profiles for people and businesses that AI systems can read: a crawlable page, schema.org markup and a plain-text card, with no login wall.
  • Seracu: Finds the searches your website is missing and writes finished articles for them in your own voice, every claim traced to a fetched page.

Guides to using AI

  • How to get AI to: A library of practical guides to getting AI to do things, and if your task isn't there, a plan for it in about ten seconds.
  • Can You Use AI: Straight answers to "can I use AI for...?": a verdict, how to do it well, and the UK rules and risks. Free, no sign-up.
  • I Want AI To: Plain-English Claude prompts, what they really produced (mistakes included), and how to put the result live from GitHub with no terminal.
  • Using AI As: Shows people in finance, HR and office admin how to use AI at work without breaking confidentiality or UK GDPR.
  • AI Guides On: Free short AI courses for people who don't code: 3 to 6 lessons, the exact prompts, and a finished thing at the end.
  • Is There a Thing: One question per page, "is there a thing for that?", with an honest yes, partly or not really, the real options, the cost and the catch.

Tools for small businesses

  • ReviewNudge: Free, no-account tools for small businesses to ask for honest reviews, reply to them and show real feedback as QR cards and testimonial images.
  • reviewsnot: One problem per page about reviews not showing: the likely cause first, the fix in steps, linked to the platform's own help pages.
  • Product Photo Shots: Send one photo of a real product and get campaign shots of that exact product in new scenes, each checked against the original.
  • Launch Video Maker: Turns a business website into a short motion reel with music for YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok. You approve the facts first.

Charts and decks

  • Look Good Tools: Makes charts and slide decks look good in your own brand: Chartsmade and Decksmade, with one login and one credit wallet.
  • Chartsmade: Turns a table, a CSV, a spreadsheet or a sentence into a finished chart you share as a live embed, PNG or SVG.
  • Decksmade: Turns a report, a spreadsheet or an old deck into a designed presentation. Every number on a slide comes from your file, and you can check each one.

Data you can search

  • MarketFrame: Free daily news sentiment for every FTSE 100 company: it reads the UK business news each morning and scores every story's tone.
  • UK Spend: London council spending made searchable: £123.3bn across more than 17 million payments from 32 authorities, by supplier and category.
  • Paws of London: A guide to dog-friendly cafés, pubs, restaurants, hotels and shops across London, plus dog walking routes.

3D and open source

  • Assets for three.js: Original 3D model packs (GLB) for three.js and WebGL: British streets, building sites and parks, in three detail levels, with a free starter pack.
  • Cortex Map: An open-source, living 3D knowledge-graph component for React and three.js, with a live demo.

The lab and side projects

  • Noctem Labs: The studio's lab: studies, failures and experiments, published with the evidence, including a museum of the fleet's own failures.
  • Jarvising (this site): An open portfolio of test projects, published as they're built.
  • Not Quite Folk: A small studio making openly AI presenter personas for Instagram and TikTok that never pretend to be human. Oriel is the first.
  • Gary the Space Horse: A bedtime show about a horse in space with a helmet and no oxygen tank, narrated by the Moon.
